Every run has 1–4 z3 queries above the current 40 M; they pass only if cvc5 finishes them first.
  • Method. A transparent proxy in front of z3/cvc5 reads the solver's resource counter (:rlimit-count, resource::resourceUnitsUsed) before and after every (check-sat); Stainless sees unchanged replies.
  • Only winners are measured. z3 and cvc5 race; the slower one is killed. cvc5 won only easy queries (≤ 0.7 M); what cvc5 needs on the heavy ones is unknown.
  • Why 40/200 is fragile. When z3 hits 40 M it gives up and the VC depends on cvc5 alone. Local runs passed 3/3; CI did not.
  • Heavy queries are all sat (unrolling steps), 10–28 s each here. The max per run ranges from 41 M to 89 M, so the tail is noisy: 200 M gives 2.2× margin over the worst of 10 runs.
  • Distribution. z3: median 0.004 M, p99 0.47 M, p99.9 3.9 M, p99.99 21 M. cvc5: p99.9 0.25 M.
  • Check on CI that z3 is 5.4.1: units are deterministic for a given query and version, not across versions.
